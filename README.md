**Career So far...**
I have recently completed (June 2024) my second postdoc from the Department of Physics, National Taiwan University, Taipei, Taiwan. The project is based on the study of exotic Higgs-Boson searches at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC), funded by the Ministry of Science and Technology (MOST), Taiwan. The main idea of the study is to get the signature of the heavy charged Higgs and neutral Higgs available to the Two Higgs Doublet Models, at the pp-collision, with a focus on the CMS detector configuration.
My earlier position as postdoc fellow was at the Institute of Physics, Johannes Gutenberg University, Mainz, Germany. The project is entitled as “Physik des Higgs-Bosons an einem zuku ̈nftigen Elektron-Proton Beschleuniger”, funded by the German Research Foundation (DFG). The main idea of the study was to establish the detailed full-simulation of the proposed Large Hadron-Electron Collider (LHeC) for the Higgs boson and other fundamental particles productions. As our first study we estimated the precision on the H → bb cross section measurement at the LHeC with a full detector simulation using the publicly available ATHENA software installed at our local cluster MOGON at our university for the study of the ATLAS detector. The work has already been published in EPJ-C. The second project includes the study of Higgs Branching Ratios (BRs) to multi-jet (¿2 jets) final state, where we work on the Higgs Effective Field Theory (HEFT) technique. This is based on machine learning approach on the fast detector simulated samples. We have archived and also communicated for publication of our study to the journal.
My Ph.D dissertation is titled, Study Of Top Quark FCNC Couplings at Future Colliders, under the supervision of Prof. Poulose Poulose at the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati, India. My research during my Ph.D based on the search of flavor-changing-neutral-current (FCNC) interactions involving the top quark and other neutral gauge Bosons of the SM. I used tools for generation of event samples through computer simulations using MadGraph5, Pythia in general and do the detector simulations depending on the detector under consideration using tools like Delphes, GEANT4, MOKKA etc. For the Physics analysis of simulated data, I used ROOT, Marlin, MadAnalysis5 (Normal and Expert mode), TMVA tools in my research. I am familiar with the C, C++ and Python computer languages and extensively used in my research. I am used to Mathematica and FORM for calculation of both analytical and numerical amplitude for Feynman diagrams of the particle physics processes. For the model based study (currently on 2HDM), I have used 2HDMC, HiggsTools etc.
